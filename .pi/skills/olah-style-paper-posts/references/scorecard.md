# Review scorecard (100 points)

| Dimension | Weight | Release expectation |
|---|---:|---|
| Source fidelity & quantified claims | 25 | Correct paper versions, shapes and cited metrics |
| Causal explanation & accessibility | 20 | Meaningful progression from problem to full model |
| Figure topology & consistency | 20 | SVG matches dataflow and captions |
| Executable demonstration | 15 | Tests validate shapes, masking and edge cases |
| CSS/HTML/responsive design | 10 | Semantic, keyboard-friendly, no mobile overflow |
| Browser QA, offline, packaging | 10 | Desktop/mobile screenshots, no console errors or dead controls |

Release gate: >=90 and **no critical correctness/security defects**. A score is a human editorial judgment, not a substitute for the automated validation report. Automatic scripts report measured checks only, not a fictitious total score.
